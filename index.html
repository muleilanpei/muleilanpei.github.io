Short Biography

I am a Ph.D. student in the Robotics Institute, Department of Electronic and Computer Engineering (ECE), The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Prof. Michael Yu Wang.

I received the B.Eng. degree in automation and the M.Eng. degree in control science and engineering from the Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT), in 2019 and 2021, respectively, supervised by Prof. Changhong Wang.

Research Interests

Intelligent Control, Reinforcement Learning, Imitation Learning, Meta Learning, Robotics.
